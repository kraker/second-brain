# Central Limit Theorem (CLT)

Sampling of means starts to resemble a [normal distribution](20251104185337-normal-distribution.md) the larger the sample size and more samples taken of a [probability distribution function](20251105120528-probability-distribution-function.md) graph. Average of means trends towards actual mean of the data set. Certain inferences are able to be made since if conditions of CLT are met, we can calculate various statistics assuming a relatively normal distribution.

This is a key concept in probability theory.

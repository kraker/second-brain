We made rocks that could think, and that wasn't enough so we made virtual thinking rocks inside of thinking rocks, and even that wasn't enough so we made nested virtual thinking rocks possible. And then we thought tupperware parties was a neat concept so we containerized the virtual thinking rocks.

And then we were like "how many digits of pi are there?" so we made thinking rocks that could all think about the same problem at the same time in a distributed way.

And very early days it wasn't thinking rocks but we blew glass tubes with a vacuum inside that could be in one of two states. Glass is still silica which is made of rocks. So it's always been rocks really.
